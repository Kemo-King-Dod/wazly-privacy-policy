# Privacy Policy for Wazly

**Effective date:** March 13, 2026

Wazly is provided by **Sada Al-Najah**.

This Privacy Policy explains how Wazly handles user information when you use the application.

## 1. Information We Process

Wazly may process information entered by the user inside the app, including:

- Names of people added by the user
- Phone numbers entered manually or imported from the device contacts
- Financial records created by the user, including debts, payments, income, expenses, notes, reminders, and related entries
- Backup files created by the user
- PDF reports generated by the user for sharing

## 2. Contacts Permission

Wazly may request access to the device contacts only when the user chooses to import a contact to quickly add a person inside the app.

Contacts are used only for this user-requested feature and are not used for advertising or tracking purposes.

## 3. How Information Is Stored

At this time, Wazly stores app data locally on the user’s device.

This includes financial records, people data, reminders, and related app content.

## 4. Backup and Restore

Wazly allows the user to create a backup of app data and restore it later.

Backup files may contain most or all data stored in the app, depending on what exists on the device at the time of backup.

Backup and restore actions are initiated by the user.

## 5. Sharing Reports

Wazly allows the user to generate and share PDF reports, including through apps such as WhatsApp.

Reports are only generated and shared when the user explicitly requests this action.

## 6. Notifications and Reminders

Wazly may send local notifications and reminders on the device for features such as payment reminders, daily reminders, and weekly review reminders.

These notifications are generated locally on the device based on user settings.

## 7. Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

Wazly does not currently use third-party analytics tools, advertising services, or tracking technologies inside the app.

## 8. Payments

Wazly does not currently process in-app payments.

## 9. Data Sharing

Wazly does not sell user data.

Wazly does not share user data with advertisers.

User information may only be shared by the user directly when the user chooses to export or share content such as PDF reports or backup files.

## 10. Data Security

We aim to protect the information stored and processed by the app. However, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting access to their own devices and backup files.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

Wazly is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected in the updated policy text and effective date.
